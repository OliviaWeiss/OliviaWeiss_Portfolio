Augmented Reality

Interaction Design • AR Experience

An augmented reality enhanced book design exploring mental toughness and emotional control in sport through interactive baseball cards and 3D visualization.

View Demo →

What

A redesigned physical book cover featuring a baseball diamond blueprint, paired with an augmented reality experience that includes a 3D baseball diamond and animated baseball cards to extend the book's themes beyond the page.

Why

This project explores how augmented reality can extend a physical book to help visualize abstract mental performance concepts in sport. While books can explain ideas like confidence and emotional control, they rarely offer an experiential way to engage with them.

Problem / Opportunity

This project investigates how metaphor and interaction can make those ideas more tangible, creating a bridge between reading about mental toughness and experiencing its principles through interactive AR storytelling.

Key Features

Augmented Reality

AR expands the physical book into an interactive experience, connecting the mental and physical sides of the game.

Strategic Visuals

Blueprint equals planning and discipline; Cards equals culture and memory; AR equals immersion and presence.

Process and Exploration

Outcome

The final experience combines physical and digital design to create an immersive exploration of mental toughness in baseball. The AR layer transforms static cards into interactive storytelling elements that help users understand the connection between mental preparation and athletic performance.
