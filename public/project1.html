Overview

An augmented reality enhanced book design inspired by Heads Up Baseball, exploring mental toughness, confidence, and emotional control in sport. A baseball diamond blueprint and interactive baseball cards use analogy and motion to connect the mental and physical game, creating an immersive experience.

What

A redesigned physical book cover featuring a baseball diamond blueprint, paired with an augmented reality experience that includes a 3D baseball diamond and animated baseball cards to extend the book's themes beyond the page.

Who

Designed for athletes, coaches, and baseball enthusiasts who are interested in the mental side of the game and want a quick, engaging preview of the book before purchasing.

Why

To provide an engaging augmented reality preview that communicates the book's core themes at a glance, helping users quickly understand its focus on the mental game of baseball while encouraging interest and purchase.

Problem / Opportunity

This project explores how augmented reality can extend a physical book to help visualize abstract mental performance concepts in sport. While books can explain ideas like confidence and emotional control, they rarely offer an experiential way to engage with them.

This project investigates how metaphor and interaction can make those ideas more tangible.

Key Features

AR

Augmented Reality
