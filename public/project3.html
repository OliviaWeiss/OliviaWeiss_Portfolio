Google doodle

Interactive Game Design

An interactive Google Doodle celebrating the Canadian Lunar Rover mission through playful exploration and symbolic design.

Watch Demo

What

An interactive Google Doodle featuring a rover that navigates the lunar surface to collect letters, with a door element symbolizing opportunity and discovery. The experience combines space visuals with game-like mechanics to celebrate the Canadian Lunar Rover mission.

Why

To celebrate Canada's historic lunar rover mission through engaging interaction that makes complex space exploration concepts accessible and playful, while symbolizing the themes of journey, progress, and the opening of new frontiers.

Problem / Opportunity

This project explores how interactive design can communicate complex themes like national achievement and space exploration through accessible, playful mechanics. By using metaphor and symbolism (the rover's journey, letter collection, and door opening), the design makes abstract ideas of exploration and opportunity tangible through interaction.

Key Features

🎮 Interactive Exploration

Users control the rover to navigate the moon's surface, creating an engaging hands-on experience that mirrors the real mission's exploratory nature.

🚪 Symbolic Door

Opening the door represents progress, opportunity, and new knowledge, symbolizing the doors this mission opens for future Canadian space exploration.

Process and Exploration

Outcome

The interactive Google Doodle successfully celebrates Canada's lunar rover mission through playful, accessible mechanics that make space exploration feel tangible and engaging.

By using metaphor and symbolism, the design translates complex themes of national achievement and scientific progress into an experience that resonates across ages while honoring this significant moment in Canadian space history.
